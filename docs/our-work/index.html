Every cart here was designed, built, and finished in our shop. Some are still in service today, which is how we know what holds up.

Project 01 — Custom Elite

Vivra Café

The build that taught us the business. A fully custom fluted bar with a curved end, bronze wordmark, and a brand mark cut from hardwood, designed around a single-group La Marzocco and a Mahlkönig E65W grind-by-weight grinder.

Designed, built, revised after inspection, permitted, and operated daily. Everything we learned here shows up in the carts we build now.

Detail That Shows

The fluting is the tell.

Each rib is cut, blackened, and wrapped around a curve. It takes longer than a flat panel and it is the first thing customers notice. This is the kind of detail Custom Elite is for.
