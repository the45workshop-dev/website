Services

Plumbing, power, and the permit process.

Building the cart is the straightforward part. Getting it plumbed, powered, and approved is where most people get stuck. We've been through it, and we'll walk you through what to expect.

Guidance, not a guarantee

We share what we've learned from our own builds and permits. We are not a licensed plumber, electrician, or permit expediter, and we cannot guarantee approval by any county or health department. Final requirements are set by your jurisdiction.

Plumbing, sink, and power

A cart that has to pass inspection needs more than a worktop. We build in the systems and talk you through how they work, so you know what you're operating and what an inspector will look for.
